      Controls
      

        

          Keyboard / mouse
          
    
            
  • WASD move
    • 
            
  • Space jump · Shift sprint · C crouch
    • 
            
  • Mouse look · Click fire · R reload
    • 
            
  • 123 / wheel weapons · Q last
    • 
            
  • Tab scores · T chat · Esc menu
    •

          Touch
          
    
            
  • Left of screen: drag to move
    •

    •

                keys move, thumb aims.

      Level designer
      

        

          Getting in
          
    
            
  • Type level design as the room code and press CONNECT.
    • 
            
  • Pick the room's width, length and height. You cannot leave that box.
    • 
            
  • Nobody else joins a design room — it is yours alone.
    • 
            
  • EXPORT gives a seed. Paste it into ROOM SEED to play the level.
    • 
          

        

        

          Building
          
    
            
  • WASD fly where you look — nose up to rise
    • 
            
  • Shift fly fast · Space/C straight up and down
    • 
            
  • Click a surface, drag a rectangle, click, then pull a height
    • 
            
  • Q then E for a floating box between two corners
    • 
            
  • Alt frees the mouse · Alt+Ctrl+click selects
    • 
            
  • F switches between boxes and ramps
    • 
            
  • 1…0 colour · T delete · G grid snap
    • 
            
  • R turns the selection, or drag one of its three rings
    • 
            
  • Tab play the room you just built · H key list
    •
